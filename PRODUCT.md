# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Kinetix serves taxi clients, independent drivers, and the app owner/admin. The primary use is mobile and task-focused: clients want to order a ride with minimal thinking, drivers want to start a shift, accept orders, see earnings, and settle payments, and the admin wants clear operational control.

## Product Purpose

Kinetix is a taxi partner app that should feel simpler than a dispatcher dashboard while keeping the full business model intact: client ordering, driver work, trial, PRO, commissions, referrals, admin control, payments, and support. Success means users understand the next action within a few seconds without losing access to any existing function.

## Brand Personality

Calm, confident, fast. The interface should feel like a modern taxi product: clean enough for daily use, vivid enough to be memorable, and practical enough for people working under time pressure.

## Anti-references

Avoid one huge admin screen, overloaded bottom navigation, walls of explanatory text, generic white cards with no product feeling, hidden functions, and decorative visual effects that make the app harder to use. Avoid making Kinetix feel like a generic SaaS dashboard instead of a taxi app.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the main path obvious: every role gets one primary action per screen.
2. Preserve all business logic: simplify paths, never remove available functionality.
3. Make the map and trip state the emotional center of the client experience.
4. Give drivers dense but calm information: order, distance, price, shift, settlement, earnings.
5. Use motion for state changes and confidence, not decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Design for mobile first, large touch targets, readable contrast, reduced motion support, short labels, and clear empty/loading/error states. Important actions must remain reachable without relying only on color or animation.
